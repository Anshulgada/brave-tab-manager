# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be kind. Assume the other person is doing their best. Keep the work about the work.

## What we expect

- Assume good faith, in issues, in reviews, and in chat.
- Give feedback on the change, not on the person.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to a feature, and ask why rather than assuming.
- Respect the privacy of other people. Do not share anyone's browsing data, vault, or keys.
- Keep it welcoming for newcomers. A first contribution rarely gets everything right.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Deliberate intimidation, disruption, or sustained pressure.
- Sexual violence of any kind.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in issues, in pull requests, and anywhere someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer through the private channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, or other contributions that break these rules, and may ban a contributor temporarily or permanently.
